The vault.

NEMO's shared library of practice scenarios, skills videos, and reference material. Hosted on Google Drive and accessible to current cohort members.

§ 01 / DRIVE
Three collections · Drive-hosted

Pick a collection.

Each card opens NEMO's Google Drive in a new tab. Sign in with your Northwestern account; if a file or folder won't open, ask a board member to verify your access.

VAULT 01

Practice Scenarios

Written scenario packets used in Sunday skills and exam prep. Mix of medical, trauma, OB, and pediatric cases — each with patient presentation, expected assessment flow, and a debrief sheet.

Open in Drive
VAULT 02

Practice Videos

Skills demonstrations recorded by NEMO instructors and curated from outside sources — airway, BVM, splinting, KED, traction, and full psychomotor walkthroughs. Watch before lab, rewatch before the practical.

Open in Drive
VAULT 03

Reference Documents

Region X SOPs, drug reference sheets, the NEMO field guide, past exam reviews, and other reference PDFs. The same documents your instructors pull from during lecture.

Open in Drive
Access
The vault is hosted across three Google Drive folders (linked above). Make sure you're signed into Google with your Northwestern email. If a file appears restricted, email nemo@u.northwestern.edu and someone on the exec board will grant access.
