Training Portal · For NEMO Students

Practice, drill, and repeat.

A working library for Northwestern's student EMTs — Region X medical and trauma protocols organized by category, plus a shared resource vault of practice scenarios, skills videos, and reference material. Built to reinforce what's taught in lecture and Sunday skills, and to keep skills sharp once you're on the rig.

§ 01 / MODULES
Explore training resources

Choose a module to start drilling.

Three modules cover what NEMO students need most: medical protocols, trauma protocols, and the shared resource vault. The protocol pages link directly to the Region X SOP document; the vault opens NEMO's Google Drive folders.

MOD 01 · MEDICAL

Medical Protocols

Region X medical protocols organized by category — cardiac, respiratory, neurological, endocrine, toxicology, OB, and environmental. Each topic opens the exact page of the official IDPH-approved SOP document.

Go to Medical Protocols
MOD 02 · TRAUMA

Trauma Protocols

Region X trauma protocols — triage criteria, adult and pediatric trauma, abuse and assault calls, and procedural references like tourniquet placement, SMR, and burn calculation. Sourced from the same SOP document.

Go to Trauma Protocols
MOD 03 · VAULT

Resource Vault

NEMO's shared Google Drive of practice scenarios, skills videos, and reference material — the same files instructors pull from during lecture and Sunday skills.

Go to Resource Vault
